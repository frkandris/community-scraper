How routing decides

  • Before generating, not after. The best model with quota left is called once — no cheap-first-then-retry cascade, which pays for the cheap answer whether or not it is kept.
  • Quota is the real constraint. Failures count against the budget too; a rejected request still consumed a slot.
  • Limits are learned. Most free tiers stopped publishing their numbers, so a 429 lowers the recorded ceiling permanently.
  • Equal quality → more quota wins. The gap between two published scores is smaller than the uncertainty in either.
  • New pages before re-runs. Re-extraction only fills quota that would otherwise expire, and only when a better model is a real upgrade (≥{{ upgrade_min_gain }} points).
